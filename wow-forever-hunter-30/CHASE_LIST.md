# Level 30 Hunter: Gear Chase List (WoW Forever)

For a **duel hunter on the Survivalist/Marksmanship build with a pet** (`sv_mm_tank`). The picks come from scoring all ~5,800 Forever items on Wowhead with `minmax.py`, using placeholder character numbers. Sources are from Wowhead's Forever database.

**[H]** = Horde only · **[A]** = Alliance only · **AH** = random-suffix world drop; buy it on the Auction House rather than farming.
Quest levels look scary (40–45), but every quest here can be **picked up at level 30 or lower** (minimum level shown). Expect to need a group or a carry for the higher ones.

## Engineering (strongly recommended)

None of these have a level requirement, only Engineering skill:

- **Trinkets:** Gnomish Net-o-Matic (210) · Goblin Mortar (205, 393–531 + 3s stun) · Gnomish Shrink Ray (205, −250 AP) · Goblin Bomb Dispenser (230) · Minor Recombobulator (140)
- **Gear:** Goblin Rocket Helmet (235) · Gnomish Harm Prevention Belt (215) · Parachute Cloak (225, +8 Agi) · Goblin Rocket Boots (no Engineering needed to wear)
- **Throwables:** Iron Grenade (175, 3s stun) · Explosive Sheep · Goblin Land Mine

## Reputations worth starting now

- **Arathi Basin:** [H] The Defilers / [A] The League of Arathor. Gives the Chain/Mail/Leather Girdles, Lizardhide Boots and the +6 Sta talisman.
- **Warsong Gulch:** [H] Kelm Hargunth / [A] Illiyana Moonblaze. Gives Scout's/Sentinel's Blade, Rune of Duty and the caster staves.
- **Darkspear Raiders / Theramore Expeditionary Force** (Honored), Darkspear Islands battleground. Gives Sta/AP and Sta/Int rings, necks and cloaks.
