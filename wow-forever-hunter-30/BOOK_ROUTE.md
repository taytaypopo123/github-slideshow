# Library Books: Horde Route (Truthseeker's Bow)

Turn books in to **Owen Thadd, Undercity**. Turn-ins are cumulative: 10 books → first quest, 20 → *Greater Friend of the Library*, **25 → Truthseeker's Bow** (that quest unlocks at **level 30**).

This route covers **26 books** (25 needed + spares), sorted for a level 29–30 Horde hunter. Locations and tips come from Patrickjarvis324's comments on [Wowhead quest 79536](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/quest=79536), collected on an Alliance level 20 paladin in beta, so they may shift by patch.

General trick: many books sit among mobs. **Die nearby, run in as a ghost, rez right next to the book**, grab it (5-second cast, interrupted by damage) and walk out.
