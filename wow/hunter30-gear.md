# Hunter 30 twink gear (WoW Forever, Horde)

For The Forever Open (Oct 17–18), level 29 → 30. Item data rechecked on Wowhead Forever on Oct 10, 2026. Community notes are from the Classic Hunter Lodge Discord (#forever-pvp and the other hunter channels). Live checklist: [Hunter 30 Twink Hub](https://claude.ai/artifact/25dBkJzqvfhrFX5WBNcmEN).

## What changed (Oct 10)

- **PvP crit damage:** the reduced crit damage in PvP is a bug, and a dev says it won't stay (pinned in #forever-pvp).
- **Oct 9 item nerfs:** many quest items were nerfed, including Outlaw Sabre, the Uldaman quest wrists and Truthseeker's Bow. Every item below was rechecked on Wowhead, and only Grimsteel Cape changed (blue → green, same stats).
- **New City of Dalaran loot:** Felbough Arm, Unstable Crystalline Shoulderpads and the Larcenist's Belt quest. Discord says the polearm was already nerfed once, so recheck it before farming.
- **Durability bug:** shooting wears down your melee weapon instead of your gun. Repair before every session.
- **Racials:** devs have hinted that Orc and Troll racials are a bit strong.

## Enchants (do last)

Chest Major Stamina (+10) · Wrist Superior Stamina (+9) · Feet Greater Stamina (+7) or Minor Speed · Hands Greater Agility (+10) · Back Minor Agility (+3) · Neck Agility (+5) · Ranged Deadly Scope (req 30). It's not confirmed yet whether Forever allows high-level enchants on level 30 gear.

## Banned at The Forever Open

Truthseeker's Bow, Flash Bomb and Goblin Sapper Charge can't be carried, not even in your bags.
